How to read the feed

  • Early wallets bought within 3 blocks of the first pool buy, or together in one block. A timing fact, not proof they are insiders.
  • Selling means tokens sent into the pool or curve. Sending to another wallet is shown apart, as "moved out".
  • Material: ≥2 early wallets or ≥5% of wallet-held supply sold on a launch worth ≥$25k, a repeat owner (≥5 earlier launches, none graduated), a graduation, and what happened 24 h later.
  • Not validated: logged for the open forward test, never posted.
