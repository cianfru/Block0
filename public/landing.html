how it works

Read, detect, follow up

01 · read

Every launch, every 15 minutes

Each Pons and Orbio launch under 72 hours old is replayed from its own transfers on the public node: who bought first, who bought together, who holds it, who is selling into the pool.

02 · detect

Only what changed, only if it matters

Two or more early wallets selling, a repeat owner with no graduations launching again, a launch graduating. Routine noise stays on the record but is not pushed at you.

03 · follow up

What happened 24 hours later

The same wallets are checked again: did they sell out, how did holders and market cap move. The update lands on the same thread, so a change is never left without its ending.

one launch, one timeline

Every change, dated, with its evidence

Each launch Block0 reads gets a timeline: what changed and when, the numbers at that moment, and the evidence behind it. That means the wallets, the amounts, the block, and where the fact came from.

Everything the agent detects is on it, whether it was posted or not, so the record cannot be curated after the fact.

what it is not

Facts, not calls

  • Not a predictor. Every test of whether these facts predict price failed or was inconclusive. The record is on How it works.
  • Not a pick list. No "gems", no ranking of what to buy, no paid placement.
  • Not an accusation. "Early wallet" is a timing fact, not proof of insider status. A sale is tokens sent into the pool; anything else is shown apart.
  • Not finished research. One open question, whether wallets with a trading record converging means anything, is measured forward in public on the radar and never posted until it passes.

On-chain facts, not financial advice. Every number is reproducible from public data; the code is open.
