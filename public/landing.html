the moat

Most launch tools are black boxes. This one shows its work.

Radical transparency is the product. A trader can't trust a number they can't check, so every score is a published formula over public data — and the model's measured accuracy is published too.

Two venues, told apart

Launchpad tokens on the Pons bonding curve — cooking, then graduated — and raw Uniswap-v4 listings that are live from block one. You always know which one you're reading.

The winner corridor

We don't just flag risk. We place a live token against the fingerprint of past winners: concrete wallet and market-cap targets per stage, with this token's path drawn through them.

Wallets across every token

Click any seller and see their whole footprint — what they hold, what they exited, whether they flip. Follow the smart money, not the influencer.

Honest by default

Every number shows its method and caveat. Estimates look like estimates. There is no buy button anywhere in this product, and there never will be.

measured, not marketed

How often it catches a winner — and how often it's fooled

The model's rule is simple: is a token tracking inside the winner corridor for its age? Here's how that rule scores — each eventual winner tested against a corridor rebuilt without it (held-out), and every loser measured against a corridor it never shaped. The gap between the two is the signal.

Loading measured accuracy…

Winners are scored leave-one-out; losers are out-of-sample by construction. A small, young cohort — every count is shown, and this is honest separation, not a long-run track record. Full table on the methodology page, re-measured on every rebuild.
