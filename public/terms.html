What Block0 is

Block0 reconstructs, from public Robinhood-Chain transfer data and the Pons launchpad's public market data, a forensic read of token launches — concentration, snipers, bundles, insider selling — and a pattern comparison to past graduations. It is a lens on what has already happened on-chain, presented honestly and with its methodology published.

No investment advice

  • Nothing here is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice, and nothing is personalized to you.
  • Scores, "blueprint fit", corridors, targets, and any framing are signal, not proof. A clean read is not a promise of safety; a strong pattern match is not a promise of success.
  • Crypto tokens — and memecoins in particular — are extremely high risk and can go to zero. Most launches fail.
  • Do your own research and never risk more than you can afford to lose.

Accuracy & limitations

  • Data is reconstructed and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Prices are swap-implied estimates, not quotes, and are pinned to the launchpad's reported market cap where possible.
  • The pattern model is built on a small, young cohort; its measured reliability is published on the methodology page and is a coin-flip in a launch's first ~30 minutes by design.
  • On-chain analysis cannot see intent — a clean distribution can still rug, and a messy one can still run.
  • The service is provided "as is", with no warranties of any kind, and may change or go offline at any time.
