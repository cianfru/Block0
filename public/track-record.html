What the numbers mean

The cost scenario applies 1% per side to indicative observed prices. It is not realized or executable PnL: gas, price impact, sellability and actual fills are unknown. Within-window highs and lows are sampled context, not assumed exits. Results do not establish an economic edge.

The legacy graduation/peak record is preserved separately and no longer collects new calls. Read the labeled legacy JSON archive. It is never combined with this record.

Offline comparisons use the same saved observations at common six-hour checkpoints, with momentum, structure plus momentum, and twenty random seeds. Their checkpoint selection differs from this transition-alert record and must be reported separately.
