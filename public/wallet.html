Reconstructing this wallet's trades…

PnL is swap-implied — reconstructed by our own engine from public pool trades, not a broker statement and not a third-party portfolio (Zerion & co. don't index this chain). Realised counts only coins actually sold; unrealised marks still-held coins at the live swap price; coins received by transfer have an unknown cost and are credited zero — so figures understate rather than overstate. Only trades on the chain's winner set are scanned, so a wallet's activity on faded/older tokens isn't shown here. Signal, not proof — never a recommendation to copy a trade.
